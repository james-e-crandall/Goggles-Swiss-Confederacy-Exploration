Swiss Confederacy Exploration Mod
Ability Summary
=========================

This summary is based on the English text entries in modules/text.

CIVILIZATION ABILITY
--------------------
Eidgenossenschaft
- Mountain tiles can be worked.
- +30% Production toward constructing the Zytglogge (Clock Tower).

UNIQUE UNIT
-----------
Pike Square
- Swiss Confederacy unique Infantry unit.
- Gains increased Combat Strength against Cavalry.
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 versions retain this ability.
- The source text contains the unresolved value placeholder +{1_Amount} Combat Strength against Cavalry.

UNIQUE BUILDINGS, QUARTERS, AND IMPROVEMENTS
---------------------------------------------
Kloster (Monestary)
- Has 1 Relic slot.

Stiftsarchiv (State Archive / Abbey Archives)
- Has 1 Great Work slot.

Fuerstabtei (Abby)
- Gains increased Culture adjacency from Mountains.
- Mountain Culture adjacency is modified by Settlement Happiness:
  - Ecstatic: +5 Culture
  - Joyous: +3 Culture
  - Happy: +1 Culture
  - Base: +1 Culture

Stadel im Sommer
- Built on non-Tundra Mountain tiles.
- Provides +1 Food.

Stadel im Winter
- Built on Tundra Mountain tiles.
- Provides +1 Culture.

Kantonalbank
- Provides +5 Gold and +4 Culture.
- Provides +1 Happiness on Quarters.

Handelsunternehme (Trading Company)
- Provides +5 Production and +4 Food.
- Provides +2 Happiness per imported Resource.
- Provides +1 Happiness per local Resource.

Rohstoffhandel (Commodity Trading)
- Imported Resources provide Culture based on Settlement Happiness:
  - Ecstatic: +5 Culture
  - Joyous: +3 Culture
  - Happy: +1 Culture

TRADITIONS
---------
Reislaeufer I
- Each Gold Resource gives Infantry +1 Combat Strength, up to +6, against Independents.

Reislaeufer II
- Each Gold Resource gives Infantry +1 Combat Strength, up to +6.
- Each Gold Distant Lands Resource gives another +1 Combat Strength, up to +6.

Grosse Glocke
- Increases Happiness adjacency and yields from Mountains.
- The source text refers to adjustable Happiness adjacency and Mountain Food, Culture, and Happiness bonuses.

Vierwaldstaetter
- Buildings gain adjacency with Lakes.

Zugewandte Orte
- Culture buildings are worth +2 GDP per turn when the player has no Alliances.

Grand Council of Geneva
- Science buildings are worth +2 GDP per turn when the player has no Alliances.

Helvetii I
- Provides +1 Happiness adjacency from Mountains.

Helvetii II
- Provides +3 Happiness adjacency from Mountains.

OTHER PROGRESSION AND GAMEPLAY BONUSES
---------------------------------------
Wars of Kappel
- Units gain +3 Combat Strength in Unfriendly Settlements following this Religion.

Worker capacity
- Provides +1 Specialist Limit in all Cities.

Mountain trade infrastructure
- Cities with at least one Mountain tile gain +10 Trade Route range.
- Those Cities also gain +1 Resource capacity.

Swiss military legacy path
- Capturing Settlements while in an Alliance awards Exploration Military Legacy points.
- This replaces the usual Deep Ocean traversal requirement described in the tutorial.

Victory tracker
- Grants 250 GDP.
- Repeatable.

GREAT PEOPLE: AMTSBURGERMEISTER
--------------------------------
General rules
- One-charge Great Person units.
- Can only be trained in a Capitol.
- The specific individual is random; each individual can be received once.
- Their cost increases with each Amtsbürgermeister trained.

Johann Conrad
- Gain 1 Relic.
- Kloster gains 1 Relic slot.

Wilhelm Herter von Hertneck
- Activates on an Army Commander.
- Renames the commander "Honorable men".
- Units within its Command Radius gain +4 Combat Strength when attacking.

Marx Röist
- Activates on an Army Commander and grants 2 promotions.

Joachim Vadian
- Provides +5% Production from Iron.

Elisabeth of Wetzikon
- Creates a Bank in a City.
- The Bank receives +4 Gold.
- Requires a tile with fewer than two Buildings and a City without a Bank.

Huldrych Zwingli
- Provides +5% Production from Iron.

GREAT PEOPLE: LEHRBEAUFTRAGTER
------------------------------
General rules
- One-charge Great Person units.
- Can only be trained in a Capitol.
- The specific individual is random; each individual can be received once.
- Their cost increases with each Lehrbeauftragter trained.

Leonhard Euler
- Activates in a foreign Capital to grant it an Observatory.
- Grants the player +1 Science adjacency with Resources and Wonders.

Daniel Bernoulli
- Activates in a foreign Capital to grant it a University.
- Grants +1 Science to all Specialists in Cities.

Paracelsus
- Activates in a foreign Capital to grant it a Hospital.
- Grants Scientific and Militaristic Attribute points.

WONDER
------
Zytglogge (Clock Tower)
- The City receives Production per turn equal to 25% of its Culture yield.

NARRATIVE AND TEXT NOTES
------------------------
- One progression-tree completion is titled "Unus pro omnibus, omnes pro uno."
- Other completion narratives describe Science or Military rewards, but the actual reward is represented by the unresolved placeholder {1_reward}.
- Several entries contain unresolved placeholders, spelling errors, and inconsistent names in the source localization.
- The text folder contains presentation, city-name, loading, and unlock text in addition to gameplay ability descriptions; those entries are not listed here unless they describe an actual bonus.
